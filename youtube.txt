TITLE
Why Did Humans Start Cooking? The Accident That Rewired Us

SHORT TITLE (thumbnail / Shorts)
WHY WE COOK

DESCRIPTION
Every animal on Earth eats its food raw. You don't. And if you tried for long,
your body would begin to fail. So what changed?

This is the story of the accident that rewired us. How a burned tuber in the
ground became a habit, how cooking moved digestion outside the body, and how the
calories and the extra hours it gave us built smaller guts, bigger brains, and
the fireside conversations that became culture. From a 1999 raw-food diet study
to million-year-old hearths deep inside Wonderwerk Cave, we follow the evidence —
and we keep the honest parts: fire didn't do it alone, the date is still argued
over, and none of it proves the very first roasted tuber. Together, it makes a
strong case that our species was rebuilt around heat.

Every animal on Earth eats dinner. Only one of them cooks it. Only one of them
stays to talk.
