# CONTINUITY — Night One

## Setting
One ordinary house, one ordinary street. Never a named place, never a brand, no signage.
Interior rooms are plain and under-furnished: a sofa, a kitchen counter, a fridge, a freezer,
a tap, a window, a hallway, a bathroom. **The fewer props, the fewer failure modes** — an
earlier video failed because a convenience store had a dozen objects per frame to get wrong.

## The single visual motif
**Red = one person.** One figure, one red shirt, everything else black line on white. The
only exceptions are diagram marks (a red X, a red level line, a red measurement tick) which
are drawn small and single.

## Register rules
- **Story frames** = inkwash, paper grain, texture, domestic clutter kept minimal.
- **Diagram frames** = flat 2D, thin lines, pure white, no inkwash texture. Used for clocks,
  gauges, timelines, distances, and the "fails in order" bars in beat 12.
- Never mix the two inside a single frame. That was the most likely failure in the previous
  video and it held up well when kept separate.

## Light discipline (the whole mood)
The video gets darker as it goes, but **slowly**:
- beats 1–2: faint grey moonlight, one sliver of leftover light in a back room
- beats 3–7: near-black interiors, single candle or phone screen as the only source
- beats 8–9: pure black exteriors, one candle-lit window
- beat 10: cold flat grey morning light
- beats 11–12: grey, plain, ordinary
Never use dramatic orange firelight or red emergency glow. That is a different, worse video.

## Continuity of objects
- The bottles filled in beat 4 must look the same when they reappear on the shelf in beat 11.
- The fridge in beat 3 (closed, cold haze) must be the same fridge in beat 10 (open, haze gone).
- The freezer stays sealed and cold from beat 3 to beat 10 — it is the one thing that survives.
- The candle appears in beat 8 (as a danger) and beat 12 (as a calm, contained thing).
