# STYLE — LOCKED (day_zero / the series)

## The look
**Clean cartoon line art, flat colour, expressive faces.** Taken from the reference
image `refs/style_reference.jpg` — the *style*, not the character.

- Thick uniform black outlines, consistent weight everywhere.
- Flat solid fills. No gradients, no texture, no painterly brushwork, no 3D.
- One simple flat shadow tone per surface. Simple contact shadows only.
- Muted, slightly desaturated palette: navy, slate grey, brick, bone, warm lamp yellow.
- Faces are the point: big oval black eyes, brows, a simple mouth. The MC is
  readable and likeable, which is what makes the horror land.

## Palette (series canon)
| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Night / interior shadow | deep navy `#16233A` |
| Concrete, walls | slate grey `#5B6773` |
| Daylight walls, paint | bone `#E8E4DA` |
| Brick, door, warmth | brick `#8C4A3F` |
| Lamp, torch, warm light | lamp yellow `#F2C14E` |
| Sick / infected skin | grey-blue `#8FA3AD` |
| Accent that means "him" | warm red `#C3432F` |

The MC keeps one red element (jacket, cap or strap) so he is findable in any frame.
Red is the only saturated colour and it appears **only** on him.

## The MC (canon, every frame)
Young man. **COMPLETELY BALD round white head — no hair at all, no spikes, no
fringe, no stubble.** Two large oval black eyes, simple brows and mouth, thin arms
and legs, plain black short-sleeved t-shirt, blue denim shorts, white trainers,
warm red hooded jacket when he is dressed for the cold.
Red is the only saturated colour in any frame and it appears only on him.

## Prompt block (prepend to every frame, verbatim)
> Clean cartoon illustration, thick uniform black outlines, flat solid colours,
> no gradients, no texture, one simple flat shadow tone. Muted palette of deep
> navy, slate grey, bone and brick, with warm lamp yellow for light sources.
> Expressive simple faces. 16:9 landscape. NO text, no letters, no numbers, no
> signage, no logos, no watermark.

## Infected (stylised, never gory)
Grey-blue skin, hollow dark eye sockets, head tilted, one shoulder dropped, arms
hanging. **No blood, no wounds, no torn flesh, no visible bite.** The horror is
posture and stillness, not damage.

## Rejected
- Black-and-white ink halftone: *"the B&W was a little too much"*.
- Woodcut, ink wash, risograph, charcoal, gouache, flat vector: all tested and out.
- Object-only framing with a tiny figure.
