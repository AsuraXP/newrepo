# PROMPT RULES — `day_one_v3`

## The one rule that matters
**The frame illustrates its own sentence, literally.** Count the people in the
sentence, then draw that many. Match the action, the direction of gaze and the
location to the words. Never improve on the narration.

v1 broke this by banning the infected and shrinking the figure.
v2 broke it by drawing two pursuers when the line says "she", and a man
*looking back* on the line "you do not look back".
v3 obeys the sentence.

## Style block (LOCKED — prepend to every prompt, verbatim)
Bold black INK COMIC panel, halftone dot texture, heavy solid blacks, white paper,
high contrast, graphic and cinematic. Deep blacks with mid-grey halftone shading.
Not a photograph, not 3D. 16:9 landscape. **No text, no letters, no numbers, no
signage, no logos, no watermark, nothing printed on any surface.**

Reference frames: `style_tests/s1_halftone_A.png`, `style_tests/s1_halftone_B.png`.

### Detail level
Keep the room readable — lamp, bed, window frame, door furniture, the things he
touches. No clutter. One object of focus per frame, the figure large enough to
read in under a second, and the infected as flat black shapes.

## Character block
Minimalist stick figure — round white head, EXACTLY TWO solid black dot eyes,
NO mouth, thin black pen-line limbs, FOUR digits per hand, FLAT BRIGHT RED
short-sleeved t-shirt with a visible hem. **Red is the ONLY colour.**

## Infected block
Tall featureless black silhouette. No face, no wound, no blood. Wrong posture
is the whole horror. Day 1 uses them twice and only at distance or through glass.

## Standing rules
1. One person per frame unless the sentence says otherwise.
2. Hands holding things fail often — prefer the object in frame, hand at the edge.
3. Screens are never legible. Abstract bars, bubbles, chips. No words, no numbers.
4. No aerial rooftops. No signage. Nothing printed on any surface.
5. If a prompt is rejected or produces a diagram, rewrite the *shot*, not the negatives.
