# CONTINUITY — `day_one_v3` (Day One, rebuilt to be grounded)

## Thesis
**No fantasy. No rule. No explanation.** The world behaves exactly like the world.
The viewer should believe they are standing in that flat with the lights off.
Nothing supernatural happens, ever. The uncanny comes only from ordinary things
doing the wrong ordinary thing — a router with no lights, a tap that stops dripping.

**Day 1 = one problem (what is happening) and one decision (go to the mall at six).**
He learns nothing all day. That is the point.

## The protagonist
Minimalist stick figure, flat red t-shirt. In **46 of 91 frames** and in at least
one frame of every beat. He never becomes a silhouette and never becomes a speck.

## The infected — deliberately scarce
Day 1 contains **one** infected seen at distance (the woman in the road, beats
6.5–6.8) and **one** silhouette at the peephole in the last frame (16.4).
No chase. No kill. No barricade breach. Day 1 is the day *before*.
The escalation ladder is now intact: Day 2 the knock, Day 3 water, Day 5 the road.

## Hard continuity
- **The mall** — ten minutes down the road, pharmacy + food hall + lit car park. Planted beats 12–14. It is the Ep 1 trap; it pays off Day 15.
- **Water** — the taps still run on Day 1 and he notices and forgets. Day 3 is when they stop.
- **The bag** — packed beat 14, still by the door in the final frame.
- **The tap** — dripping for two years, stops in beat 15. Never mention it again until Day 3.
- **Adeyemi** — voice only. Never seen until Day 2.
- Numbers: 12:58, 01:20, 03:00, 06:00, 08:00. Never shown on screen, always spoken.
