TITLE
Day One: They Never Run and They Never Get Tired

DESCRIPTION
At 11:48 on a Thursday the lights go out and the phone stops being a window. By midnight there is a car in the road with its door open and nobody coming back for it. By two in the morning there is a woman in your hallway calling the same name, eleven times, and then stopping in the middle of it.

By eight there is a neighbour at your door saying the same sentence every forty seconds, word for word.

Day One is not the fight. It is the day you find out that staying inside works, and then open the door anyway.

Nine places, one red shirt, and a steel pipe. The horror is not gore — it is posture. They do not run. They do not hurry. They are never out of breath, and you always are.

Sources: Ready.gov and FEMA power-outage guidance, CDC emergency water supply guidance.
