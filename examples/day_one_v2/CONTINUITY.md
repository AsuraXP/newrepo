# CONTINUITY — `day_one_v2`

## The building
One apartment block. The story happens in: **the flat**, **the landing outside**,
**the stairwell**, **the fire escape**, **the roof**, and **the street below**.
Nothing else. No other locations, ever.

## The protagonist
Minimalist stick figure, flat red t-shirt with a visible hem. He appears in
**64 of 97 frames**. He is often large in frame, angled, in motion. He is not a
silhouette and he is not a distant speck — that was v1's mistake.

His escalation across the episode: **scrolling → standing still → barricading →
swinging a pipe → sitting on a roof.** Track his posture, not his feelings.

## The infected
Featureless black silhouettes. Same three or four of them throughout:
- **The woman from the hall** — one arm hangs low and out of time. Beats 6, 7, 13, 14, 15, 16. She is the one who chases him.
- **Mr Adeyemi** — behind the door in beats 8 and 9. Never clearly seen.
- **The one in the kitchen** — through the bathroom window, beat 17. Down in one swing.
- **The crowd** — beat 18 and 19, dozens, in the street, all facing the building.

Never show: a bite, a wound, blood, a face on any of them. They are shapes.

## Objects that must not change
- **The chain** — brass, on the inside of the front door. Beats 10, 16.
- **The barricade** — bookcase + table on its side + chair under the handle. Beats 10, 12, 13.
- **The pipe** — long steel, taken in beat 11, used in beat 17, still in his hands on the roof.
- **The phone** — plain black, pale screen, never legible. Battery falls 71 → 43 → 34 → 12.

## Light
Beats 1–7 night, phone-lit. Beat 8–13 flat grey morning. Beat 14–17 hard
overcast, high contrast. Beat 18–19 open grey sky, the brightest frames in the
episode — which is the point: he gets out into the light and it is worse.

## The voices (voices.json)
- Narrator, second person — `en-US-AndrewNeural`
- Mr Adeyemi, through the door — `en-GB-RyanNeural`, -4%. Calm, level, no distress.
- The woman from the hall — `en-GB-SoniaNeural`, -6%. Patient, unhurried.

The forty-second loop in beat 8 is the scare. `sfx` cues (knock x3, thud, rumble)
are synthesized by `cartoonstyle.sfx` and mixed at stitch time.
