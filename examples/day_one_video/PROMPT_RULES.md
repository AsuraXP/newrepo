# PROMPT RULES — `day_one` (Day One, Ep. 1 of the series)

Locked look. Every prompt in `visuals.csv` is the SHOT description only; prepend
the style block below at generation time.

## Style block (copy-paste)

```
HARD RULES: Style B&W inkwash on white, high contrast, thick black line, paper
grain. MEDIUM IS WET BLACK INK ON PAPER - loaded brush strokes, bleeding edges,
NOT clean architectural line drawings. 16:9 widescreen. NO text, no letters, no
numbers, no signage, no logos anywhere.

PEOPLE: minimalist stick figures - round white circle head, EXACTLY TWO black dot
eyes, NO mouth, thin black pen-line arms and legs, simple mitten hands, NO double
outlines (no trousers, no shoes). The protagonist wears a FLAT RED SHORT-SLEEVED
T-SHIRT with a visible hem at the hip. Red is his only colour, and there is at
most ONE other red accent in any frame.
```

## Hard bans

- No text anywhere. Numbers live in the audio, never on the frame.
- No blood, no gore, no bodies, no wounds.
- No zombies on screen. Not once. The series shows *consequence and absence*,
  never the creature. This is the whole visual thesis: the horror is a locked
  door and a voice, not a monster.
- No second character's face. Adeyemi is a shape in the peephole, a voice, a pair
  of shoes under a door. Keep him unseeable - it protects later episodes.
- No phone UI legibility: bubbles, bars and batteries are plain shapes, no glyphs.

## Frame size

Native 1376x768, generated landscape. Do not resize. The stitch stage snaps to
even dimensions and pads to a uniform canvas; never upscale.
