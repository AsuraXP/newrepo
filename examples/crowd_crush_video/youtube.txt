TITLE
The Crowd Doesn't Trample You. It Kills You Standing Up.

DESCRIPTION
Nobody in a crowd crush is running. Nobody is even screaming. They are standing upright, awake, and suffocating — because the crowd has stopped being a crowd and become a fluid.

Crowd deaths are almost never caused by trampling. They are caused by compressive asphyxia: the pressure of packed bodies prevents your chest from expanding, and you suffocate on your feet. At six people per square metre you can no longer choose where you go. Most people have no idea that this is the mechanism, which is exactly why they don't leave early enough.

This is the part of survival nobody teaches: the four moves that buy you air, and the one test you can do in the dark to know if you are already in danger.
