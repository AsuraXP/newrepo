# CONTINUITY — `crowd_crush`

## Series continuity (Saiki Survivors)
- **Saiki** = minimalist stick figure, flat RED t-shirt, no pants, no shoes. Appears in every
  episode as the demonstration body. He is *not* a survivor-hero in-fiction; he is the
  recurring "this is you" figure. Never give him a face beyond two black dot eyes.
- **No injury state carries between episodes.** This is explainer, not serial fiction.
- **Red = Saiki + one diagram arrow.** That's the whole color vocabulary. Locked series-wide.

## Per-video state
- **Crowd density is the only state variable**, and it is expressed by *gap between bodies*:
  - sparse (2/m²): clear white gaps, arms swinging → beats 2.4, 2.5, 12.2
  - tight (5/m²): shoulders touching, arms pinned → beats 2.6, 3.2, 3.4
  - fluid (6+/m²): bodies merging into lines, no gaps → beats 2.7, 6.1–6.4
  These three densities must look **clearly different** from frame to frame. If 2.6 and 3.4 look
  the same, the film stops explaining anything. QC this specifically.
- **Saiki's stance progression** (this is the spine of the video):
  - Beats 1–6: passive, upright, arms at sides, being moved.
  - Beats 7–9: active, boxer guard, feet apart, resisting nothing.
  - Beats 10–12: grounded, curled, then free at the rim.
  Never show the boxer guard in beats 1–6 (it undercuts the whole hook).
- **Diagram frames** (2.3–2.7, 3.5, 4.1–4.7, 6.1, 8.6, 11.2, 11.4, 12.3) are a *separate visual
  register*: flat 2D, thin lines, on pure white, no inkwash texture behind them. Mixing the two
  registers inside one frame is the most likely failure in this video.
