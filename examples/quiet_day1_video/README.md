# quiet_day1_video

Ep. 01 — day one, one room, one decision.

- **Problem:** the room has one exit, and the exit is a corridor full of things that
  follow sound.
- **Decision:** the door (company, or a trap) or the window (a drainpipe in the dark).
- **Answer:** the window. It is the last line of the episode, not a speech about it.

Narration is 803 words / 75 sentences, so **75 image moments**, ~3.9 s per image,
**4:52** at the 2.75 w/s planning pace, 8 batches of 10.

## Rules for this one
- No text in any frame. The phone screen, the note on the counter, the alarm clock:
  abstract bars and chips only.
- One person in frame, always the same one: never named, never seen from the front.
- The standing figures are posture, not threat. No impact, no wounds, no blood.
